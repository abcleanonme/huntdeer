**Description** (4000):
For decades you hunted the deer. Now the deer hunts back. Sort of. Mostly it hides in bushes.

Huntdeer is a goofy 3D stealth game where you play the animal and the hunters are the obstacle. Sneak through four woods, finish two tasks in each, then slip out the exit before anyone gets a clean shot.

PICK YOUR CRITTER
- Doug the Deer sniffs out every hunter and trap.
- Bun Jovi the rabbit hops over everything.
- Pepé Le Nope clears a clearing.
- Bearnard knocks hunters flat with one roar.
- Sir Quacksalot flies over the trees, if he dares in duck season.

MEET THE HUNTERS
Bowhunter Bob, Rifle Randy, Shotgun Sheila, E-Bike Brad, Ghillie Gus (he is a bush), Cooler Carl (he is napping), Trapper Tammy, Drone Dan and Houndsman Hank. Every hunter is rolled fresh with a name, an outfit and a stat they won't stop bragging about.

BOOP THEM
Sneak up behind a hunter and boop them. Knock their hat off and add them to your Hunter Log with their weight, height and Doe & Crockett score. Boop a tree stand and see what happens.

SECRETS
Something in every woods doesn't belong. Find all four and follow the trail somewhere the hunters really don't want you to go.

- 4 woods plus a hidden one
- 6 critters to unlock
- 34 achievements
- Built for iPhone and iPad
- No ads, no tracking, plays offline
