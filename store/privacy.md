# Huntdeer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Huntdeer does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- The game makes no network connections. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
- Your progress (stars, unlocked critters, Hunter Log, achievements, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
- The game does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/abcleanonme/huntdeer/issues
