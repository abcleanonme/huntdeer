# Huntdeer Support

Stuck behind a hunter? Found a bug? Open an issue at https://github.com/abcleanonme/huntdeer/issues and include your device (iPhone or iPad model) and iOS version.

**Saves:** progress lives on your device. Deleting the app erases it.

**Controls:** left side of the screen moves (push the stick all the way to sprint), drag on the right side to look. JUMP, BOOP and your critter's ability are the buttons on the right.
